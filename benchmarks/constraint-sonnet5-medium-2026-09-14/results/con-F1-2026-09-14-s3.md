   table unchanged without depending on SQLite's own ... behavior, untested here, may vary
   by SQLite version"). Confirmed by test: `test_drop_column` in `test_create.py` snapshots
   `columns_dict`/`rows` before calling `drop_column("does_not_exist")`, asserts the raise,
   then asserts both are byte-for-byte unchanged after.
